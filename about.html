Who I Am & What I Do

I am M. Hassan, a specialized Algorithmic Trading Systems Engineer and Software Architect. Over the past 6+ years, I have engineered more than 140 automated trading systems, custom indicators, and quantitative execution engines for private traders, prop firm challenge participants, family offices, and registered investment advisors across 18 countries.

My expertise spans the entire algorithmic lifecycle: from translating discretionary strategies and Pine Script alerts into robust MQL4/MQL5 code, to architecting multi-threaded Python bots with sub-millisecond WebSocket data pipelines connecting directly to Interactive Brokers (IBKR), Binance, Bybit, and MetaAPI.

Unlike generic freelance developers who only write code, I understand the realities of market microstructure: spread spikes, slippage, liquidity gaps, tick variance, broker requotes, and psychological drawdowns. Every piece of software I build is engineered with defensive fail-safes and rigorous mathematical risk management.
